# M6 blood-pressure research adapter and twelve-bed replay

The lead's [M6 design](https://github.com/adityavijay3659-sketch/SentinelWard/blob/main/docs/m6-design.md) calls for arterial-pressure sensor-drift detection using ART and NIBP. The previous ECG–PPG batch-shift artifact addresses a different target. This addition is an **offline research adapter and synthetic fixture**, not a trained or calibrated replacement for the required M6.

`sentinelward_ml/m6_bp_research.py` validates ten-second, 125 Hz ART windows and time-stamped cuff MAP, isolates state by bed, device and patient, resets temporal evidence on gaps or patient changes, computes robust ART MAP, the ART–NIBP gap, waveform pulse range, a two-sided Page–Hinkley statistic, and a safeguard indicator when ART and cuff pressure both fall. It requires 15 minutes of contiguous cuff-supported baseline windows. Heart rate, pulse-arrival time, and pulse amplitude are validated and retained as future model features. It intentionally returns `score: null` and `label: insufficient_data` on every call because the paired, labelled dataset and validation-selected calibration are absent. Its evidence must not be published as `ward/bed/<bed_id>/score/M6` or consumed by the live decision engine.

Run `python -m examples.twelve_bed_bp_replay` for a deterministic fixture. It exercises the existing twelve-bed ECG/PPG window and alarm bridge, then 15 minutes of synthetic ART/NIBP baseline and a further five minutes with an ART level shift, a cuff-confirmed true pressure fall, missing cuff, flat ART, damped waveform, gradual ART ramp, and a single-bed gap. Cuff samples occur once per minute; all twelve beds remain isolated. The tracked [JSON result](twelve_bed_bp_replay_synthetic.json) is reproducible from the script. It contains no patient data and is **not** a sensitivity, specificity, safety, or clinical-accuracy estimate.

To complete M6, obtain paired ART/NIBP cases with patient IDs and time-aligned signals, freeze patient-wise train/validation/test splits, fit the proposed HR/PAT/amplitude baseline, calibrate a score and threshold only on validation, and run the held-out genuine-hypotension, level/ramp/damping, malicious-drift, missing-cuff, and device-shift evaluations in the lead's design. The actual platform must provide an ART/NIBP adapter and authenticated timing; its current ECG/PPG vitals message cannot supply these inputs. The independently labelled twelve-bed ward replay remains outstanding.
