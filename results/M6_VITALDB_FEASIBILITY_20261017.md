# M6 public ART/NIBP data audit — 17 October 2026

The [official VitalDB dataset API](https://api.vitaldb.net/) exposes case and track tables from 6,388 surgical cases. In a read-only census, 3,035 case track lists contained both an arterial waveform (`SNUADC/ART`) and cuff MAP (`Solar8000/NIBP_MBP`). The [source probe](m6_vitaldb_source_probe_20261017.json) records the exact counts and a limited case-10 timing example. These are **track-presence counts**, not verified paired signal windows. The first case-10 cuff chunk held a repeated displayed value; that pattern cannot establish new cuff measurement times. The first arterial chunk also requires waveform decoding and quality checks before use.

The [feasibility notebook](../notebooks/05_m6_vitaldb_feasibility.ipynb) runs [`m6_vitaldb_audit.py`](../examples/m6_vitaldb_audit.py) in Colab. It freezes a subject-disjoint candidate split from publisher metadata, selects twelve distinct training subjects, and audits actual waveform and displayed cuff-value coverage sequentially. It saves only audit summaries to Drive and reports an ETA. Its waveform stage has **not yet been executed**; no twelve-case coverage result, drift accuracy, or validated M6 score is claimed.

Even a successful technical audit cannot make this a live model: VitalDB is an intraoperative surgical dataset, not a target-ward dataset; repeated cuff displays lack verified measurement timestamps; and independent arterial-sensor-drift and genuine-hypotension labels are absent. The existing synthetic twelve-bed replay checks software isolation, not clinical performance. M6 must continue to return no live score until these gaps, validation-only calibration, held-out patient evaluation, and target-device acceptance are resolved. The monitor's own alarms must remain independent.
